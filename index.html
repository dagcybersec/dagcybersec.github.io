About Me

I’m a cybersecurity practitioner focused primarily on penetration testing and offensive security. My experience has been built through consistent hands-on practice across Linux, Windows, Active Directory and web application environments, supported by structured penetration testing reports and practical security projects.

I’m particularly interested in understanding how systems work beneath the surface. This curiosity led me into cybersecurity and continues to drive how I learn, investigate and approach unfamiliar technologies.

Alongside offensive security, I maintain practical exposure to defensive security, including log analysis, monitoring and investigation, which helps me understand attacks from both perspectives.
